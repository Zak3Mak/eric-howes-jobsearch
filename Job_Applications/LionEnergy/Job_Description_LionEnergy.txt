ABOUT THE ROLE
Launching a new U.S.-based battery manufacturing line. Hands-on Production Manager partners with engineering to set up and optimize the line, then owns it in operations: scaling output, hiring and developing the team, running daily production; establishing processes and best practices for quality, safety, efficiency.

Production leadership: own output, yield, quality, uptime; track OEE, throughput, scrap, downtime; execute to schedules.
Process & CI: standardized work instructions and SOPs; lean and continuous improvement; root cause and corrective action; reduce waste.
Quality & compliance: QC processes for battery assembly; regulatory requirements; documentation, traceability, reporting.
Equipment: operate, troubleshoot, maintain production equipment; preventive maintenance; line optimization and scaling.
Team leadership: build, train, lead production team; staffing, scheduling, performance.
Safety: battery-manufacturing safety (chemical, electrical); OSHA compliance; audit-ready environment.

PREFERRED
