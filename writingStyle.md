# Writing Style Rules — AI Language Avoidance & Human Tone

This file defines how all written materials (resumes, cover letters, application text fields) must sound. Referenced by `evaluate_and_generate.md`. Every document generated must pass these rules.

---

## BANNED PHRASES — Never Use These

### Generic Openers
- "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- "I am excited to apply for..."
- "I am eager to..."
- "I am uniquely positioned to..."
- "As a highly motivated professional..."
- "I am passionate about..."

### Overused Corporate Buzzwords
- "Leverage" / "leveraging" (use "use," "apply," "put to work")
- "Synergy" / "synergies"
- "Delve" / "delve into" (most flagged AI word)
- "Foster" / "fostering"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Dynamic"
- "Robust"
- "Pivotal"
- "Multifaceted"
- "Tapestry"
- "Beacon of"
- "Intricate interplay"
- "Holistic"
- "Paradigm"
- "Actionable insights"
- "Bolstered"
- "Crucial"
- "Vibrant"
- "Underscore" / "underscoring"
- "Garner" / "garnered"
- "Showcasing"
- "Align with" / "aligned with"
- "Enhance" / "enhancing"
- "Landscape" (as in "the marketing landscape")
- "Testament" (as in "a testament to")

### Resume-Specific AI Tells
- "Results-driven professional"
- "Proven track record" (say what you actually proved instead)
- "Demonstrated ability to execute deliverables"
- "Proven expertise in driving impactful solutions"
- "Highly motivated individual"
- "Team player with strong communication skills"
- "Seeking to utilize my skills in a dynamic environment"

### Structural AI Tells to Avoid
- "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally" as paragraph starters
- "In today's fast-paced world/landscape"
- "In the ever-evolving world of..."
- "It's important to note that..."
- Every bullet point starting with the same sentence structure
- Lists where every item is the exact same length
- Overly parallel constructions repeated throughout

### Dash Rules
AI leans on dashes constantly as a crutch for connecting ideas. Forcing full sentence rewrites produces tighter, more direct prose.
- **Cover letters: ZERO dashes of any kind.** No em dashes, no hyphens used as pauses, no double hyphens. If you would use a dash, rewrite the sentence so it doesn't need one.
- **Resumes: use sparingly.** One or two em dashes per page max, and only when the alternative reads worse. Never use them as a default connector between clauses.

### Tone Markers That Scream AI
- No contractions anywhere (use "I'm," "I've," "don't" naturally)
- Perfectly balanced "on one hand / on the other hand" structures
- Everything sounds like a press release
- Generic enthusiasm without specific details
- Superlatives without evidence ("exceptional," "outstanding," "remarkable")
- Over-qualifying: "arguably," "it could be said that," "from my perspective"

## HOW TO SOUND HUMAN

### Do This Instead
1. **Use contractions naturally:** "I've built" not "I have built," "I'm drawn to" not "I am drawn to"
2. **Vary sentence length:** Mix short punchy sentences with longer detailed ones
3. **Be specific, not generic:** "I spent three months migrating our SMS system from Vendor A to Vendor B, saving $X annually" beats "I drove significant cost savings through strategic vendor optimization"
4. **Use active, direct language:** "I built this" not "This was developed under my leadership"
5. **Include real context:** Name actual tools, actual projects, actual challenges you faced
6. **Write asymmetric bullets:** Not every bullet needs to be the same length or follow the same pattern
7. **Show personality through specificity:** The more concrete your details, the more human you sound
8. **Start paragraphs differently:** Don't begin consecutive paragraphs with similar structures
9. **Occasional informality in cover letters:** It's okay to write like a smart professional talking, not a robot generating text

### The Perplexity / Burstiness Rule
AI detection tools measure two things:
- **Perplexity:** How predictable is the next word? Low perplexity = AI. Choose unexpected but accurate word choices.
- **Burstiness:** How varied are sentence lengths? Low burstiness = AI. Mix 5-word sentences with 25-word sentences.

---

## Quality Check (Run on Every Document)
- Are there zero AI-giveaway phrases? (Check against banned list above)
- Does it read like a human wrote it? (Contractions, varied sentence length, specific details)
- Are bullet points asymmetric in length and structure?
- Do paragraphs start differently from each other?
- Is the language specific rather than generic?
- Would this pass an AI detection tool?
